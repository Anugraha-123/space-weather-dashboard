🛰️ About Space Weather

Space weather describes changes in the space environment caused mainly by solar activity. Solar flares, solar wind and geomagnetic disturbances can affect satellites, radio communication, navigation systems and other space-based technologies.

📡 Data Source

Live data is obtained from the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center .

Monitoring includes:

• Planetary Kp Index
• Solar Wind Speed
• Solar X-ray Flares
• Geomagnetic Activity
